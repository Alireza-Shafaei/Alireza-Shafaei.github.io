I work where a learning system meets the hardware and the people that depend on it. My question is usually the same one in different clothes: when a model, or a model's output, makes a claim, how do we check it cheaply and with a known error rate?

That has meant a cloud language model whose every decision passes a small verifier before it runs, a privacy audit that found a published attack flagging far more genuine samples than it promised, and an evaluation protocol for deployed models under drift that was fixed before any result was seen.

ResearchProjects by stage

Final stageMaster's thesis, 2025 to present

In-Context Learning as a Service: Lyapunov-Optimized Split Computing

A quantized cloud language model decides, through retrieval-augmented prompting, where deep networks should be split across heterogeneous edge devices. Because its suggestions cannot be trusted blindly, every decision passes through a deterministic guardrail that certifies memory feasibility on both sides before anything runs.

  • Proved that the guardrail terminates in a bounded number of steps and only adds a finite constant to the drift-plus-penalty bound, so safety and queue stability hold together.
  • Roofline-based GPU energy model, shown to be strictly convex and non-monotone in clock frequency.
  • NP-hard transmit-power subproblem solved with a fractional-programming method that converges monotonically.
  • Validated on two physically separate machines over a rate-shaped uplink, so every number comes from hardware.

Journal manuscript in preparation for IEEE Internet of Things Journal. Supervisor: Dr. Hamed Shah-Mansouri

In progressResearch project, 2026 to present

Where Do Decomposed Diffusion Models Leak Membership?

The first controlled privacy study of wavelet diffusion and diffusion-GAN models: 11 pretrained generators across pixel, wavelet and latent families, tested with 13 published attacks and with CBT, my own member-free test that is calibrated per wavelet band and noise level.

  • The design was pre-registered and time-stamped on Zenodo before any result was seen, with blinded splits and low false-positive-rate evaluation.
  • Found that a published attack promising a 1% false-positive rate flags 14.5% to 27.2% of genuine samples.

Target venue PoPETs 2027. Supervisor: Dr. Sajjad Amini

Preprint, under reviewResearch project, 2026

Retrain, Alert, or Rollback? Production ML Under Concept Drift

When a deployed model's data drifts, should the system retrain, raise an alert, or roll back? We describe the answer as a five-state machine in which each response has its own trigger and cost, with joint retraining gates, a two-stage canary deployment and a rollback whose type depends on the cause of the drift.

  • I built the evaluation harness, the data-provenance pipeline and the archived artifact.
  • Pre-registered protocol over 13 real data streams with rank-based analysis, so no method could be tuned to the test afterwards.

Joint first author. Supervisors: Dr. Babak Hossein Khalaj and Dr. Hamid Hadibeik

Proposal stageFollow-up to the thesis, 2026

Sense, Predict, React: A Brain-Inspired, Risk-Controlled Edge–Cloud Uplink for IoT Event Detection

An IoT node that decides when to send, what to send and when to act on its own. It uses a retina-like event encoder with homeostatic thresholds, a spiking predictor whose prediction error is both the uplink message and the anomaly evidence, and a local reflex that is always verified by the cloud.

  • Online risk control bounds missed events under censored feedback through randomised audits, and a Lyapunov virtual queue enforces a byte budget.
  • Planned matched and ablated comparison of spiking, dense and activity-sparse models on TSB-AD-M and ESA-ADB over a real-link testbed.

Supervisor: Dr. Hamed Shah-Mansouri

CompletedBachelor's thesis, 2024

Reinforcement Learning and Intelligent State Discretization

Tabular Q-learning needs a discretised state space, and a uniform grid is too coarse where it matters and wasteful everywhere else. I replaced it with a refinement driven by the agent's own visit counts. Without any model of the physics, the grid settled on the balance point of CartPole and the valley walls and summit of MountainCar. Benchmarked against PPO on CartPole, MountainCar and Pendulum.

Supervisor: Dr. Saeed Bagheri Shouraki
